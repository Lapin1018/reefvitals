Notes — water changes, additives, equipment changes, observations, and retests

Free worksheet from ReefVitals · lapin1018.github.io/reefvitals · Record results as reported by your test kit. This sheet does not prescribe target ranges or doses.

Make the record useful.

  1. Label every reading. Include the date, time, units, and test kit or meter used. Don't mix salinity in ppt with specific gravity, or alkalinity in dKH with meq/L.
  2. Note what changed. Record water changes, additives, equipment changes, and observations alongside the test results.
  3. Verify surprises. Check your test kit instructions and repeat an unexpected result before using it to make a change. Follow product directions and retest before dosing again.
  4. Keep the history together. Compare like-for-like readings over time. Choose a testing routine appropriate to your tank and its livestock.
